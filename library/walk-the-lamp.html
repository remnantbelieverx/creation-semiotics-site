What this sheet is for

Give one guided tour of the seven positions so a reader can:

  1. Name each Spirit-lens in plain words.
  2. Open one free first stop per branch.
  3. Use the three bilateral pairs without inventing filler.
  4. Know what not to manufacture — especially on Counsel.

The public claim

The lamp is a coordinate system, not a content dump. Seven positions under one fire: threshold, reception of what is, skilled making, the servant center, sight after the crossing, friendship-counsel, and co-regency under the Lamb.

The Branch pages already hold the public triads and free cards. This sheet is the touring order and the discipline — how to walk.

Discipline to teach out loud

  1. Allowlist before invention. No public card without a row. The lamp shows books; it does not authorize new filings.
  2. Thin Counsel is rigor. Do not manufacture Position 6 cards to match Knowledge’s count.
  3. Precision over resemblance. If it only “feels like,” it does not file. Cup, knob, and flower are three named forms of one hammered work — a reading grammar, not a botanical proof.
  4. The hidden counsel stays off this internet. Depth that belongs in intimacy is not sold and is not posted here.
  5. The gospel is never paywalled. Verdict and Care stay free.

A short teaching sequence

Thirty to forty-five minutes.

  1. Open the lamp; name the seven lenses.
  2. Walk one pair live — 1 ⇌ 7 or 3 ⇌ 5.
  3. State the empty-shelf rule for Counsel.
  4. Assign one free entry and one Branch page.
  5. Close with Isaiah 11:2 or Revelation 4:5 — one fire, ordered gifts.

Questions for a small group

  1. Which position names your current pressure — threshold, construction, or commission?
  2. Why might a thin shelf be more faithful than a full one?
  3. How does the unpaired center change how you read the six?

Overclaims to refuse

  • The Menorah map replaces exegesis of particular books.
  • Every doctrine has a forced triad slot.
  • Public depth includes what the vault withholds.

Allowlist A-12 and the public Branch / entry cards already named. Not a corpus filing. Not a dump of the twenty-two triad lock tables.
