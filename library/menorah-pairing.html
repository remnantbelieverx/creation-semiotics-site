What this sheet is for

A working map of the lamp’s second dimension — pairing — so a reader can:

  1. Name the three height-pairs and the unpaired center.
  2. Read Isaiah 11:2 as one Spirit with ordered designations, not a grab bag.
  3. Navigate the public site by pairs without inventing filler.
  4. Leave the full lock table in the vault.

The public claim

The Menorah is not six random arms. Exodus specifies branches out of both sides of one shaft. That shaft is not a seventh peer; it is the source and axis. The public Library uses that bilateral map: Wisdom faces Understanding, Knowledge faces Counsel, Fear faces Might.

The linear walk asks where am I in the sequence? The pair walk asks who faces me across the fire? Both questions belong to one object. Empty public shelves — especially Counsel — are sometimes discipline, not neglect.

Isaiah 11:2 — public altitude

“And the Spirit of the LORD shall rest upon him, the Spirit of wisdom and understanding, the Spirit of counsel and might, the Spirit of knowledge and the fear of the LORD.”
Isaiah 11:2

The Spirit is one. The designations are ordered, not a grab bag. Wisdom and understanding is the most explicit grammatical couplet in the verse, and it matches the lamp’s inner height-pair.

Do not flatten the rest of the verse into the height table as if every “and” were already a branch facing its neighbor. The lamp’s pairs are physical (same height across the shaft). Isaiah’s grammar is a second, related reading of the same sevenfold Spirit. Both belong to one object. The full couplet analysis stays in the vault.

Precision: do not force every modern spiritual-gift list onto the Menorah. Stay with Isaiah’s text and the lamp’s architecture.

A short teaching sequence

Twenty to thirty-five minutes.

  1. Open the lamp; touch three pairs and the center.
  2. Read Exodus 25:31–32 — six branches out of the sides of one shaft.
  3. Read Isaiah 11:2 slowly, without rearranging the “ands.”
  4. Walk one pair live: Wisdom and Understanding.
  5. State the empty-shelf rule for Counsel.
  6. Close: one light; faces across the fire.

Homework: the public pairing entry, then one free Sign on Knowledge.

Questions for a small group

  1. Why is an unpaired center different from a seventh equal branch?
  2. Where do we treat topics as disconnected when the lamp treats them as pairs?
  3. How does “Friend, not servant” (Counsel) resist method-only religion?

What we claim · what we refuse

Bilateral pairing on the lamp is independently specified in Scripture. Opposite-arm pairing on a snowflake is derived from radial six-fold geometry — see MS-01 and Entry 2.1. Do not treat those two pairings as the same kind of fact.

Overclaims to refuse

  • Every doctrine has a forced cup–knob–flower slot.
  • Pairing justifies numerology games.
  • A thin public Counsel shelf means the vault is empty of counsel. It may simply be withheld.
  • Publishing the eight-lock table under the name “pairing.”
