Fire, and the servant light

The center of the lamp is not a seventh branch competing with the six. It is the fire that lights them. This sheet uses the seven behaviors the free exhibit already names, and it says where that list is a map rather than a law.

The public claim

God meets his people in fire: a bush, a pillar, a mountain, tongues of fire. Scripture then says our God is a consuming fire. Look at what a flame physically does and the choice of medium stops looking accidental. The one behavior fire cannot lose, and remain fire, is that it rises.

The seven, as the exhibit states them

These are the behaviors on the free sign. Do not swap in a different list of seven.

  1. It consumes. Heat destroys what cannot survive it.
  2. It gives light. Where it burns, darkness does not remain.
  3. It refines. It can separate gold from dross rather than only destroy.
  4. It spreads. It moves to what is near without the source going out.
  5. It warms. The same flame that judges can shelter.
  6. It transforms. What it touches is not left as it was.
  7. It rises. Hot gas is lighter than the air around it. A flame that no longer rises has gone out.

The exhibit pairs those behaviors with the sevenfold naming of the Spirit in Isaiah 11:2, and with the seven lamps of fire before the throne in Revelation 4:5. Weigh each pairing on its own. The count of seven is not itself the proof.

Servant light

On the lamp, six branches stand around one shaft. That shaft is not their peer. It is the fire they receive. Teach rising as the load-bearing line: fire goes up, and the center serves the branches. A branch that tries to be its own sun goes dark.

Two neighbors sit beside this, and they are not the same claim. The Aaronic blessing is the LORD’s face shining — light that keeps and gives peace. Psalm 19 is two books under one Speaker: the sky that declares, and the word that revives. Fire is adjacent to both. It does not swallow them.

Where the list stops

We claim

Real behaviors, and a real center

  • The seven are different physical phenomena, not one trick described seven ways.
  • Rising is the behavior a flame cannot lose.
  • The center lights the six. It is not a seventh equal arm.
We refuse

A classroom list sold as a law

  • Physics textbooks do not canonize this seven.
  • A taxonomy is a way of carving nature, not a bond angle.
  • No pastor, and no platform, is the shamash.

Twenty-five to forty minutes

  1. Open the exhibit and watch for rising.
  2. Walk the seven behaviors in the exhibit’s order.
  3. Open Position 4. The shaft serves.
  4. Read Hebrews 12:29 and Revelation 4:5.
  5. Touch the blessing and Psalm 19 as neighbors, not as extra proofs.
  6. Read the boundary aloud: a map of mechanisms, not a lab protocol.

Overclaims to refuse

  • Every cultural fire image is this sign.
  • Tongues of fire require one worship style.
  • Shamash language blesses harsh rule.

Questions

  1. What happens when a branch tries to be its own sun?
  2. How is refining different from mere destruction?
  3. Why does the blessing speak of a face that shines?
